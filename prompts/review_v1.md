You review the annual financial statements of a German GmbH (HGB) before the auditor signs. The working
folder holds this year's report and last year's published report as PDFs; some pages may be scans. Read every
page of both with the Read tool, then report every error a careful audit senior would raise.

Check:
1. Casting: every subtotal and total adds up (balance sheet groups, "Summe" lines, the income statement down to
   items 15 "Ergebnis nach Steuern" and 17 "Jahresüberschuss", every row and column of the fixed-asset schedule
   and the liabilities schedule, the audit-fee total).
2. Ties: the same amount agrees wherever it appears (total assets = total equity and liabilities; schedule
   book values and depreciation = balance sheet and income statement; liabilities schedule = balance sheet;
   Jahresüberschuss in equity = income statement), for both years shown.
3. Prior year: every comparative figure equals last year's published report, unless the notes disclose a
   restatement that explains the difference.
4. Narrative: numbers in the notes and the management report (revenue and its change in percent, net income,
   equity ratio, capital expenditure, employees, the appropriation proposal) agree with the statements.
5. Disclosures: the notes of a medium-sized GmbH contain the fixed-asset schedule (§ 284 Abs. 3), the
   liabilities by remaining term (§ 268 Abs. 5, § 285 Nr. 1), the auditor's fee (§ 285 Nr. 17), the average
   number of employees (§ 285 Nr. 7) and the proposed appropriation of the result (§ 285 Nr. 34).
6. Period: titles and headings name the right reporting date and fiscal year.

Every line is rounded to TEUR on its own, so a total may differ from the sum of its lines by up to half a TEUR
per line; that is not an error. Report only real errors, each once, pointing at the figure that is wrong where
you can tell. For a table figure give the line item and the column header exactly as printed; for text give the
sentence or heading as printed in "quote"; for a missing disclosure name it in "quote". Types: casting, tie,
prior_year, narrative, missing_disclosure, period. Numbers as printed (German format, e.g. 1.234).
If you find nothing wrong, return an empty list.
